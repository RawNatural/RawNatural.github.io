About

Why I Build

I believe the way humanity moves is about to fundamentally change. Electric vertical takeoff and landing — quiet, clean, autonomous flight — isn't just a technology trend. It's the beginning of a shift as significant as the automobile. The idea of rising above a city without noise, without traffic, following the light — that's not science fiction. People are building it right now, and I intend to be one of them.


My background is deliberately broad because I think the best engineering leaders understand the full picture. I've managed a $50M infrastructure programme. I've laid composite airframes by hand in a workshop at midnight. I've written smart contracts and full-stack web applications from scratch. I did an exchange at TU Delft and completed their Urban Air Mobility certification. I'm a MENSA member, a Half-Ironman finisher, and a snowboard instructor. I work hard, I care about quality, and I want to build something that matters.

Aerostructures · Team Leadership

UQ Space — Rockets & Rover

4 years building rockets and rovers with UQ Space. Led the aerostructures team to 1st place at AURC 2025 (5,000ft category) and served as Deputy Project Lead for 2nd place at the Australian Rover Challenge 2025 across a 40-person team. Manufacturing · Composites · Precision Machining

Composite Airframes & Metal Fabrication

Fiberglass/epoxy composite airframes built using vacuum bagging over precision mandrels and two-part female moulds — including a seamless nosecone process I developed for a perfect surface finish. Aluminium and steel fabrication including the MECH3100 gearbox tractor (designed and built solo within $400) and the original UQ Space rover chassis, still operational 5+ years on.

Industry · Programme Management · Infrastructure

Siemens Mobility — $50M Railway Signalling

9 months as Project Engineer on the Wellington rail network resignalling programme — replacing legacy mechanical interlocking with Westrace MkII and ETCS across the entire network. Managed the BOM end-to-end, chaired procurement meetings, generated installation requirements for 168 axle counters, and led the sole-effort close-out of a long-stalled post-commissioned project (T2UH) that multiple engineers before me had been unable to resolve — achieving full client-signed certification before the end of my placement.
